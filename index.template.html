Super Simple Speedo was built with one goal: to create a clean, reliable speedometer without the clutter.
Free forever. No ads. No subscriptions. No account. Just a simple speedometer with automatic speed limit detection where available.
Built with the help of modern AI tools, but carefully designed, tested and refined by a human who believes simple software is often the best software.
Driving safely and respecting speed limits is always the responsibility of the driver.
Made in Switzerland 🇨🇭
Your journeys are your business.
Super Simple Speedo does not require an account and does not collect or sell your personal data.
Any optional statistics are stored locally on your device and never leave it.
