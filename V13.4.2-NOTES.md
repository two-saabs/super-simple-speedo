# Super Simple Speedo v13.4.2

## Experimental mode cleanup

- Reframes **Super Simple Journey** as the recommended all-in-one transport experiment.
- Reframes **Transport Detective** as a standalone detector-only alternative.
- Journey automatically uses the Transport Detective engine internally; users no longer need to enable both switches.
- The two visible choices are mutually exclusive: choosing one turns the other experience off.
- Turning Journey off also turns off its underlying transport engine, avoiding a hidden detector state.
- Journey continues to replace the detector badge with the session timeline while active.
- Adds clearer settings cards and concise `Recommended · all-in-one` / `Standalone` labels.
- No transport-classifier thresholds, walking detection, GPS handling, timetable matching, or Journey segmentation rules were changed.
